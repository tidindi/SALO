PR and communications in Norway for international brands

SALO is an independent, senior-led PR and communications agency in Norway. We advise international brands and organisations on how to reach Norwegian audiences — and handle the delivery ourselves.

01

Entering the Norwegian market

Global campaigns rarely work in Norway without local adaptation. The media landscape is compact and closely connected, journalists are sceptical of marketing language, and material that works in larger markets can easily become noise here.

That is where we come in.

We identify the angle that holds up editorially, determine what is worth pitching — and what is not — and create Norwegian-language material that feels native to the market. We then handle the media relations and press work ourselves.

Some clients use us for a single launch or campaign. Others retain us as their local PR and communications partner in Norway.
